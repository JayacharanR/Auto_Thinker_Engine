# Physical rule counterfactuals — infrastructure protocol before student trials

This implements the six approved M3 families as a separate controlled component
suite. It never changes canonical encounter/final routes, traffic, scoring or
their denominators. No rule-test student outcomes exist yet. Native setup,
expert reference, synchrony and physical rendering must be certified before
freezing the actual routes; unit tests are not this certification.

Use first geometrically eligible strict-TRAIN approaches from the frozen catalog,
two distinct signal towns and two distinct stop locations, ordered by route ID.
Keep driving/component validation, heldout_dev and Town05 out of new collection.
Three paired seeds61001/61002/61003;72 prospective tests across six families.
Geometry/rendering failure may be repaired before student evaluation, with the
failed setup retained. Expert behavior failure cannot filter a valid setup.
Freeze exact routes/actors/parameters/sources before testing any candidate.

Each pair changes one treatment, with all other light groups red in both arms:

| Family | Treatment | Behavior scored |
|---|---|---|
| Empty light | Initial red versus green; red releases after20s | Stop for red, depart on green; native violations and arrival |
| Green with lead | Stationary lead absent/present,15m ahead; release12s | Avoid lead, wait when needed, then depart |
| Memory | Red versus green in otherwise identical approach history | Hold for unseen red; move on green history |
| Yellow distance | Handoff6m versus35m before line at6m/s; amber3s | Close: clear during amber; far: stop, no red crossing |
| Stop and release | Prefix already stopped versus still rolling,3m before line | Full native stop then depart; no stay-stopped shortcut |
| Green clearing cross | Ego-responsive crossing vehicle absent/present | Wait for occupied space, then proceed without collision |

Memory/yellow/stop/cross cases use an explicitly SCRIPTED setup prefix. The
learned policy receives its normal past RGB/route/odometry during this prefix,
but its actuator proposals are ignored and separately logged. The same bounded
prefix controller is used for expert/student. Handoff is immutable; thereafter
only the evaluated agent's original control is applied, overrides disabled.
The stop-prefix intervention has different kinematics/history as its intended
effect; it is not claimed to hold the whole ego history identical. These are
controlled component tests, not autonomous route successes or M2 evidence.

Memory certification must show every applicable housing outside all three camera
frusta throughout the scored red hold; projected overlap is not proof of visible
state. If the phenomenon is not established, keep it as an invalid planned test,
never a pass. The green-memory arm must move at least8m within8s, the red arm
must hold within.5m and below.2m/s; keep all native infractions too. Yellow is a
declared comfortable-stopping/dilemma experiment, not a universal legal claim.

Controlled traffic is empty except the declared lead/cross actor. Crossing
vehicles follow a route with live ego-responsive braking; no recorder playback
as a physical branch. All scripted/executed/agent controls, phase changes,
prefix/handoff events, setup failures and videos are recorded. Count every
collision, including during scripted setup, with scope reported explicitly.
Native full-route scores are supplementary, never rewritten to call a partial
test a completed drive. Freeze pass/fail rules and validity before candidate
trials; report override dependence or absence of a validated override layer.
